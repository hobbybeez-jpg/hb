🐝Receiving Tracker · Hobby Bee

Scan or type a UPC to see which shipments it belongs to (open shipments first) and where it is in Renfrew stock.
Change Password
Requires the manager password (admin password; if not set, Manager 1 or Manager 2 password works). New passwords need at least 8 characters. Leave the staff password blank to not require one (not recommended: anyone with the link could then open the app).
Shopify
—
Signs in to Shopify and reads products and transfers. Nothing is changed.
Workflow
1 Inventory staff import the shipment (it appears here automatically) → 2 Warehouse counts and taps "Qty Confirmed" → two parallel lanes: [3 Manager 1 confirms inventory (password) → 4a Manager 2 lists online (password)] and [4b Renfrew staff count and put away] → 5 once both lanes are done, Renfrew staff scan Pending items onto the store floor; the shipment is then hidden.
When Shopify is connected: step 4a also creates a Shopify transfer Marine Drive → Renfrew (in transit), and from step 5 Renfrew staff marks it received with "Confirm transfer received" (the quantities counted in 4b).
