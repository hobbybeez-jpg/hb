🐝 Receiving Tracker

Scan or type a UPC to see which shipments it belongs to (open shipments first) and where it is in Renfrew stock.
Change Password
Requires the manager password (admin password; if not set, Manager 1 or Manager 2 password works). Leave the staff password blank to not require one.
Workflow
1 Inventory staff import the shipment (it appears here automatically) → 2 Warehouse counts and taps "Qty Confirmed" → two parallel lanes: [3 Manager 1 confirms inventory (password) → 4a Manager 2 lists online (password)] and [4b Renfrew staff count and put away] → 5 once both lanes are done, Renfrew staff scan Pending items onto the store floor; the shipment is then hidden.
